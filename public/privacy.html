Privacy Policy — Band Rehearsal Helper

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Band Rehearsal Helper ("the app") is a free, open-source web app for viewing and managing a band's song repertoire. This page explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

Who runs this app

The app is developed and maintained by Michael Peteichuk. Contact: michael.peteichuk@gmail.com.

How the app works

The app is a static, client-side-only web page with no backend server. It reads song data from a Google Sheet that you choose to connect (you supply its ID in the URL). All data processing happens in your own browser.

Google Sign-In and what it's used for

Signing in with Google is optional and only needed if you want to add or edit songs directly from the app. If you sign in, the app requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • Your email address — used only to show who is signed in and to resolve edit permissions.
  • Google Sheets access — used only to read and write rows in the specific Google Sheet you connected, so the app can display songs and, if you have edit rights on that sheet, let you add or update them.
  • Google Drive metadata (read-only) — used only to check whether your Google account has edit access to the connected sheet, so the app can decide whether to show the "Add/Edit song" controls.

The app never requests access to any other file, sheet, or Drive content beyond the one spreadsheet you explicitly connect.

Where your data is stored

There is no backend and no database operated by the app. Your Google access token, email, starred/pinned songs, and preferences (e.g. dark mode, hidden chords) are stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. Nothing is sent to or stored on any server controlled by the app's developer.

Signing out revokes the Google access token immediately. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything the app has stored locally.

Third parties

The app communicates directly with Google's own APIs (Google Identity Services, Google Sheets API, Google Drive API) to perform the functions described above. The app does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and does not share your data with any other third party.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy
