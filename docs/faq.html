Frequently asked questions

How the Google Sheet, the metronome, setlists and your local data actually work.

Getting started

What is Band Rehearsal Helper?

A free, open-source web app for bands: lyrics with chords, custom setlists, and a built-in metronome. There's no backend — it's a static app that reads your songs straight out of a Google Sheet you already own.

Do I need to install anything, or does it cost money?

It's free and open-source, with no sign-up required to just browse songs. It runs in any browser, and you can also "Add to Home Screen" on iOS or Android to use it like a native app.

Does it work offline?

Yes — once a song list has loaded, the app keeps working without a signal. The only thing that needs a connection is fetching fresh data from your Google Sheet.

Songs & the Google Sheet

How do songs get into the app?

The app reads a public Google Sheet directly from your browser — no API key, no backend. Add or edit rows in the sheet and they show up in the app right away. See the README for the exact column layout.

What's the difference between Pinned songs and Favorites?

Pinned songs (PinedByDefault in the sheet) are pinned to the top of the list for everyone. Anyone can locally pin/unpin any song too — that only changes the order for you, it's never written back to the sheet. SortingPriority controls the order among pinned songs (lower first); unpinned songs stay alphabetical.

Favorites are your own locally starred songs. Once you star anything, the prev/next buttons on a song page cycle through your favorites instead of the pinned list.

How does Smart Search work?

It searches song name, lyrics, labels, GroupBy, language, and setlists all at once, is tolerant of typos, and highlights and ranks matches by precision.

How does chord parsing and transposition work?

Chords are parsed straight out of the lyrics text — just make sure there are clear spaces between chord symbols in the sheet's Text column. Once parsed, you can transpose a song to any key with one tap.

What are the YouTube / Chordify / Holychords links?

If a song's row in the sheet has a YouTube, Chordify, or Holychords link, the app shows a quick shortcut to it on that song's page.

What's Admin Mode, and how do I get edit access?

Sign in with Google from the app. If your account has edit access to the connected sheet, an "Add Song" button and a per-song edit (pencil) icon appear, letting you create and update songs without opening the spreadsheet. Deleting a song from the UI isn't implemented yet — do that directly in the sheet for now.

Metronome

How does the built-in metronome work?

It's a local Web Audio metronome, preselected from the song's own BPM and TimeSignature columns. It supports simple (2/4–5/4), complex (5/8, 7/8, 8/8, 10/8, 11/8, 13/8), and compound (6/8, 9/8, 12/8, and their ♩. triplet-feel variants) time signatures — clicking every eighth note at its correct note-value-scaled interval for /8 meters, rather than treating them like /4.

Pick any accent pattern the time signature offers (Off, On, or a meter-specific grouping like 3+3+2+2+2), and any of several click sounds.

When do my tempo/signature/accent/sound changes actually get saved?

Only once you tap Save — that becomes the song's remembered metronome preset. Tweaking settings without saving is discarded when you leave the song. Reset clears the saved preset back to the song's own sheet defaults.

Can I pick a different speaker for the metronome's clicks?

Yes — from the speaker icon in the metronome, or from Settings, you can route its clicks to a specific output device (e.g. a USB interface or Bluetooth speaker) instead of your system default. This is Chromium-only (Chrome/Edge, desktop or Android). If the picked device isn't connected, playback falls back to the system default and flags that; it automatically redirects (or stops) if the device connects or disconnects while playing.

Setlists & your local data

What are Setlists?

Your own local playlists built from any songs in the catalog, switchable from the bottom tab bar (Library/Setlists). Open a setlist to browse, add, or remove songs; opening a song from inside one makes prev/next cycle that setlist's order instead of your favorites. New songs are always appended at the end.

How do I reorder songs inside a setlist?

Pick a song up by its grip handle and drag it to a new spot — the rest slide out of the way live, and the order is only committed once you let go. You can also just type a position number into a song's row to jump it straight there.

How do I share a setlist with the rest of the band?

Share a single setlist via its row's icon, or select several from the Setlists page (Export → checkboxes → Export) to bundle them into one file — optionally including Line/label notes, Tonality/Capo choices, and Metronome presets for just those songs.

Importing a file whose setlist name already exists on this device asks Cancel / Merge / Replace per setlist (with "Apply for all"). Merge adds the imported data on top of what you already have, letting the imported value win only where something overlaps.

What are Line & Label Notes?

Tap any lyric/chord line, or a section label like "Chorus"/"Intro", to attach a personal cue (e.g. "I come in here"). It shows up right away, inline under the line or label — stored locally in your browser only, per device, never written to the shared sheet.

Can I back up or transfer my local settings?

Yes, from Settings — export Favorites, Pinned overrides, Tags, Tonality/Capo choices, Line notes, Setlists, Appearance defaults, and Metronome presets as a single JSON file, choosing exactly which categories to include. Importing only lets you enable categories the file actually contains, and merges with what you already have rather than overwriting it.

Does my local data sync automatically between my devices?

Not yet — Favorites, Pins, Setlists, Notes, and Appearance are stored per device in your browser's local storage. Use Settings → Backup to export from one device and import on another in the meantime. Automatic multi-device sync is on the roadmap.

Appearance & viewing

Can I change the font size, column layout, or hide chords/lyrics?

Yes — Settings lets you set a preferred font size and column layout (e.g. split a song into Verse/Chorus columns), remembered separately for portrait and landscape, with a live preview. You can also toggle chords or lyrics off entirely for a cleaner view. Zooming or changing columns on one song's own page only overrides that song, leaving your global default untouched.

Does dark mode follow my device automatically?

Yes, it follows your OS light/dark theme setting automatically — handy on a dim stage or in a bright room.

What languages is the app's interface available in?

English, Українська, Polski, 日本語, Español, Português (Brasil), हिन्दी, and বাংলা — defaults to your browser/OS language, switchable anytime from Settings → Language. Only the app's own UI chrome is translated; your song content (lyrics, titles, tags) always stays exactly as entered in the sheet.

Can I print my lyrics and chords, or share a song?

Yes — there are clean print styles for a paper copy, and a Share option for a specific song or a filtered list.

Privacy

Where is my data stored, and what does signing in with Google share?

There's no backend database — everything local (favorites, pins, notes, setlists, preferences) stays only in your own browser, on your own device. Full details, including exactly what Google Sign-In requests and why, are in the Privacy Policy.
