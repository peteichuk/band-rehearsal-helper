Everything a rehearsal needs

Add a song to the sheet and it's instantly live — no setup, no admin work.

Google Sheets as your database

Add, edit, or reorder songs in a spreadsheet you already own — changes show up instantly, no deploys.

Smart chords & one-tap transposition

Chords are parsed from your lyrics automatically and can be transposed to any key on the fly.

Built-in metronome

Pre-fills BPM and time signature from the song's own data — tap to keep time while you play.

Installable & works offline

Add it to your home screen on iOS or Android. Once loaded, your setlist works without signal.

Auto light & dark mode

Matches your device's theme automatically — easy on the eyes on a dim stage or bright room.

Custom albums & setlists

Build your own setlists, drag to reorder, and share one as a file with the rest of the band.

Admin mode, no spreadsheet-wrangling

Sign in with Google — if you have edit access to the sheet, add or edit songs right from the app.

Personal cues & notes

Tap any line to pin a private reminder — "I come in here" — visible only to you, on your device.

Print-ready lyrics & chords

Clean print styles for a paper backup, plus quick links out to YouTube, Chordify, and Holychords.

Up and running in three steps

No sign-up, no server to maintain — the sheet is the whole backend.

1

Create a Google Sheet

List your songs with lyrics, chords, BPM and links — one row per song.

2

Share the app link

Point the app at your sheet's ID and send the link to your bandmates.

3

Rehearse

Browse, star favorites, build setlists, and tap the metronome to keep time.

Ready to run your next rehearsal from one link?

It's free, open-source, and there's nothing to install on your end.
